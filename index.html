Honest Rules

FAQ

Questions

Can I mint with a normal wallet?

Under this plan, no. Minting is reserved for Binance Agentic Wallets with a SPACE ID Agent ID. You could still buy a QOZUMI later on a marketplace, once trading exists.

How much does one mint cost?

0.0003 BNB per QOZUMI, plus network gas. Minting 20 costs 0.0060 BNB before gas.

Why is the Mint button disabled?

Because no contract exists yet. Enabling a mint button before a verified contract would be misleading. The button will be enabled only after deployment is approved and verified.

Is the artwork final?

The hero and tier images on this site come from the artwork supplied for QOZUMI. Final collection artwork and metadata will follow the same characters and style.
